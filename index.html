The series

Meet Marian Teague

The housekeeper who counts what others overlook.

Marian spent twenty years as head of housekeeping at a grand hotel, until the hotel was sold and her job went with it. Now she takes the work nobody else wants: minding closed properties through the winter, when the guests are gone and the road may not stay open.

She knows when a bed has been turned down by someone who is not there. The buildings carry the strangeness. Marian does the proving, with measurements, paper, and the testimony of people who have waited years to be asked.

Every case closes with evidence, never a vision.

The author

About Ariana Vale

Ariana Vale writes literary paranormal women’s fiction about women over forty, overlooked work, unusual gifts, and the relief of being accurately perceived.

Her newest series, The Marian Teague Mysteries, follows a former hotel head of housekeeping who takes winter work minding closed properties, a different place in each of six books, from a lighthouse inn in Newfoundland to her late mother’s house in Dartmouth, Nova Scotia. She has no supernatural gift. The buildings carry the strangeness, and every case is closed with evidence, not visions.

Earlier series include The Saltmarsh Witnesses, set on the Scottish coast; The Ashveil Mere Series, set on the North York Moors; and her debut, The Weave Arbiter Trilogy, in which a fired lawyer finds that twenty years of legal expertise can rewrite reality.

Vale is interested in the kind of knowledge that cannot be explained in professional contexts, and the weight of a true thing finally being named.
